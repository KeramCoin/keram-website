Crypto: Washington moves, but the final rules still wait.

United States senators moved the CLARITY Act forward. The bill could define more clearly which crypto assets fall under securities rules and which under commodities rules. A final Senate vote is expected after the August recess.

Why it matters: Clearer rules could make it easier for builders, companies and everyday users to understand the ground beneath their feet.
